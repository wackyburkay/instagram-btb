How do I find someone's user ID?
  1. Open a private window: Ctrl+Shift+P in Floorp or Firefox, Ctrl+Shift+N in Edge or Chrome.
  2. Go to instagram.com/username/. You don't need to log in or close the cookie prompt.
  3. Press Ctrl+U to view the page source.
  4. Press Ctrl+F and search for profile_id. The number after it is the ID, for example "profile_id":"25025320".
  5. Paste that number into the box above and click Find.

Alternatively, a lookup site such as commentpicker.com can find the ID from the username. Using external websites sends the username to those sites.
